# ChitwanFit — Final Deliverables

## 1. Startup Name
**ChitwanFit**

## 2. Value Proposition
We help aspiring Ironman athletes, who are overwhelmed by the distances and scattered training,
climb from Sprint to Full Ironman step by step with a free, stage-based workout tracker —
with progress bars, calorie estimates, a Nepal leaderboard, and a live training map.
