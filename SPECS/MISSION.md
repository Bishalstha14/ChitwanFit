# Mission

## What is this project?

ChitwanFit is a free fitness training web app for athletes working toward real
Ironman triathlon stages: **Sprint → Olympic → Half Ironman (70.3) → Full
Ironman**. Each stage combines swim, bike, and run distances. Users log
workouts, track pace and calories, climb stages, and compare on leaderboards.

---

## Fill This In (Your Startup's Mission)

**Startup Name:**
> ChitwanFit

**The Problem:**
> People who want to become "Ironman" athletes don't know where to start. The
> distances are intimidating, their progress is scattered (or not tracked at
> all), and there's no simple free way to see which real stage they're ready
> for or how they compare to others.

**Target Audience:**
> Beginning to intermediate runners, cyclists, and swimmers who dream of the
> Ironman but need a free, simple, step-by-step path. Starts local, grows global.

**Your Solution:**
> A free web app that shows the real Ironman stages, lets you log swim/bike/run
> workouts (distance + time), calculates pace and calories, tracks your
> progress toward the next stage, gives diet tips per stage, and shows
> leaderboards so athletes can compete for fun.

**Value Proposition (one sentence):**
> "We help aspiring endurance athletes who are overwhelmed by Ironman distances
> by providing a free, stage-by-stage training tracker, helping them climb from
> Sprint to Full Ironman with clear progress and friendly competition."

---

## Non-Negotiables

- The product must be explainable in 20 seconds or less.
- The app is **free for everyone** — no payments, ever.
- The landing page must have one clear Call to Action (CTA) — not two, not three.
- Every design decision must trace back to the brand personality words.
- Keep everything simple and beginner-friendly: Flask + SQLite, just like the
  students who will use this project learn.
- The site is served over the Codio public URL, bound to `0.0.0.0`.

---

## Out of Scope

- No payments or monetization.
- No user login system or passwords for this version (we keep it simple).
- No complex APIs, ORMs, migrations, or advanced frameworks.
- No React, Vue, Angular, or npm packages — plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Flask.
